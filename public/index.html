About Me

I’m a Lead Systems Engineer with 20+ years of experience across site reliability engineering, platform operations, and observability tooling, most recently at Amadeus, working within high-availability global airline reservation infrastructure.

I have also delved, as part of that experience, into the ITIL model realm dealing with incident, change, and problem management, and led teams in a 24/7 environment to maintain 99.x% availability for production mission-critical systems.

My focus is on making systems observable and incidents boring: alerting-as-code with Prometheus and PromQL, Grafana dashboards, and helping design and build systems that are centered on prevention and self-recovery and actually reach the people who actually get things corrected, with git-based workflows for infrastructure changes, and clean incident routing through ServiceNow or another incident management system. I hold a MS in Computer Information Systems from Nova Southeastern University and a MBA from the University of Florida, and I’m a U.S. military veteran.
